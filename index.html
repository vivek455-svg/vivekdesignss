About me

Vivek SarojWeb designer & Framer developer

I'm a web designer and certified Framer expert based in Delhi. I design sites for SaaS and tech teams who need their website to explain the product quickly and earn trust on the first visit.

I start with the message, not the mockup. Once we agree on who the page is for and what it should get them to do, the layout, visuals and motion all follow from that decision.

Because I design and build, what you approve in Figma is what goes live. You get a fast, responsive Framer site your team can update without waiting on a developer.
